Whereday 2.0 — map-first reviewer notes (local draft)

Previous 2.0 candidate: build 63. Its App Store version slot was renamed to 1.8.4 for the startup patch, recorded as waiting for review on November 8. Preserve that patch. Restore a 2.0 slot and confirm a new candidate build with the later hardening before applying these local notes.

This version is a full visual redesign of the iPhone app, widgets, Live Activity, and Apple Watch app. The tabs are Map, Atlas, Places, and Archive. The app opens on a full-screen map; pull up the day sheet for stops, moves, and the day dial. Switch between today, yesterday, the last 7 or 30 days, all history, or a calendar range. Replay a day on the map at 1×, 10×, or 60×. Settings opens from the gear on Archive. Onboarding is now three steps; the "How much should it remember?" step only chooses existing distance-filter and auto-stop settings.

No Whereday account or demo login is required. Core location history remains on-device. Location permission and purchases are separate: purchasing never grants location permission. Photos remain in the Photos library and automatic photo matching is optional.

The paywall is now a three-page carousel, but purchase behavior is the same as the released 1.8.3 version: new users see the required tracking paywall and can select Individual or Family, then Weekly, Yearly, or Lifetime. Apple controls purchase confirmation and trial eligibility. New in 2.0: right after a free trial starts, the app asks for notification permission and, if allowed, schedules one local reminder two days before the trial ends. The reminder is removed if the trial is canceled or Lifetime is bought. Restore Purchases, Privacy, and Terms remain accessible. Existing verified original builds through 50 retain their legacy access; the eligibility cutoff remains 51. Existing Lifetime purchases remain valid. Expired new-cohort subscribers can still view and export existing records, while new recording requires active access. No product IDs or App Store price schedules are changed by this update.

Custom onboarding and purchase analytics remain removed. Optional myself.md purchase claims remain disabled. Functional export scheduling and user-configured webhooks remain available. No new data types are collected.

The App Store screenshots are design illustrations of fictional sample history, rendered from the “One Long Day” design artifact. They are not captures of a customer’s location history. The artwork covers iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Duo and the product page header; overlay copy and drawn app labels are localized in eleven locales.

Please keep this version on manual developer release after approval.
