§ 02 — What it does

An instrument, not a feed.

01

A journal that writes itself

While tracking is on, arrivals and departures are logged as visits, with when you came and how long you stayed.

02

Routes, recorded

Record walks, rides, hikes and road trips with the full GPS line, start and end points, distance and duration.

03

Private by default

Stored on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no Whereday server receiving your location.

04

Easy on the battery

Start and Stop, distance filters and auto-stop timers keep precise sessions from running longer than you meant.

05

Export and import

Eight export formats, including GPX, with daily files and optional scheduling. Supported Whereday files and Google Timeline JSON import free.

06

Apple Watch recording

The Watch app records routes on its own, even with your phone left at home. Complications show today at a glance.

07

Daily timeline and outings

Visits and movement merge into one day. Trips away are grouped into outings you can rename, annotate and replay.

08

Correct a visit

Confirm or fix a detected stop with nearby suggestions, undo any change, and save places that name future visits.

09

Photos on the map

Opt in and geotagged photos appear beside the visits where you took them. Photo files never leave your library.

§ 04 — Private by construction

There is no Whereday server to send it to.

Privacy here isn't a setting you have to find. Visits and routes stay on your iPhone unless you export them or point a webhook at a receiver you choose. Apple services provide place names and map features. Production version 1.8 disables onboarding and purchase analytics; earlier versions can still send the technical events described in the policy.

QUESTIONS

Will Whereday drain my battery?+

Whereday uses iOS location services and gives you controls such as Start and Stop, distance filters, and auto-stop timers. High-accuracy route recording uses more battery, so use it for intentional sessions like trips, walks, hikes, or site visits.

Where is my location data stored?+

Your visits and routes are stored locally on your iPhone. Whereday does not require an account and does not store your location history on Whereday servers.

Does Whereday use Apple geocoding?+

It can, if you choose features that turn coordinates into readable place names or addresses. Those geocoding requests go through Apple's system service and are governed by Apple's privacy policies, not a Whereday location database.

Can I delete my location history?+

Yes. Delete data from the app settings. Since Whereday does not keep a server copy, deleting the app also removes the local data from that device unless you exported or backed it up first.

Can I export or import my history?+

Yes. There are eight export choices, with content depending on record type. OwnTracks and Overland carry GPS points. Import accepts supported Whereday JSON, CSV or Markdown visits and points, plus supported Google Timeline JSON. GPX, KML, GeoJSON, OwnTracks and Overland exports are not Whereday restore files. Supported import and Export Preview are free; export access follows the new-user and existing-user rules. Keep original files and check the export/import reference.

Does Whereday work with Apple Watch?+

Yes — and the Watch app records independently. It keeps its own GPS session, works without your iPhone nearby, and queues routes offline until it can sync them back. Watch complications also show today's visits, distance, and tracking status.

Does Whereday sync with iCloud?+

No. Whereday stores your journal locally and does not sync through iCloud. Export and import are the portable backup path, and the Apple Watch syncs directly with your iPhone rather than through the cloud.

What is free and what is Pro?+

The download and supported import are free. New users joining with version 1.8 need an eligible trial, subscription, or Lifetime purchase to begin tracking. Existing users retain free tracking and their earlier export purchase requirements; prior lifetime purchases remain valid. Current U.S. Lifetime prices are $49.99 Individual, $89.99 Family, and $40.00 for an eligible Family Upgrade. Apple shows local prices, trial eligibility, and renewal terms before confirmation. See the complete terms.

Does Whereday work with OwnTracks, Overland, or Dawarich?+

Whereday can POST GPS points to a configured receiver. An Overland batch sent by the released native webhook code was stored in Dawarich 1.18.0 and survived a receiver and database restart. Physical recording and unattended delivery remain separate checks. Webhooks follow your export access and are off by default. Follow the checked Dawarich setup, or choose another records workflow.

What can Siri and Shortcuts do?+

Start and stop tracking, read saved visits and outings, rename journal records, ask for today's stats, or export a chosen date range as a file. Use Whereday actions in Shortcuts; built-in Siri phrases cover recording, stats, renaming, and Export Data. Test a shortcut manually before scheduling it. See the action list and export recipe.

Can exports run automatically?+

Yes. Auto Export saves your day to a folder you pick, once daily or as often as every hour, keeping one consistent dated file per day. Wake-ups are best effort: background refresh plus a scheduling service that sends timing-only pushes and never sees your location.

Is Whereday a Google Timeline alternative?+

Yes. Whereday records your own visits and routes without a Google account. Google Timeline also stores history on your device and offers encrypted backup; Whereday differs in account requirements, export formats, and recording workflow. Whereday 1.8 can preview supported Google Timeline JSON and import visits and timed points. Keep the original archive: selected records do not reproduce every Google activity or replace its device-transfer backup. Follow the import walkthrough or read the comparison.
