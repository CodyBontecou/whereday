# Make sense of your location files

Practical tools for checking your own files before importing or converting them.

By [Cody Bontecou](https://isolated.tech), Whereday’s developer · Reviewed November 20, 2026

## [Visited countries map](https://whereday.com/tools/visited-countries-map/)

Turn a GPX, KML, GeoJSON, Google Timeline JSON or Country Days CSV file into a shaded world map with a count and a per-country list of first and last dates. Add or remove countries by hand, then save the map as SVG or PNG and the table as CSV.

Borders and coastlines are simplified, so points near them are listed rather than guessed. Your files stay in your browser.

## [Merge GPX files](https://whereday.com/tools/merge-gpx/)

Combine several GPX files into one, such as a week of daily exports. Keep tracks separate, join them as segments or make one continuous line, drop exact duplicate points, and check the summary and preview before you download.

Then [open the result in the GPX viewer](https://whereday.com/tools/gpx-viewer/). Your files stay in your browser.

## [KML and KMZ viewer](https://whereday.com/tools/kml-viewer/)

Open a KML or KMZ file and see its placemarks, lines, polygons and timed gx:Track points, plus a list of what the viewer skips. Your file stays in your browser.

Then [convert it to GPX](https://whereday.com/tools/kml-to-gpx/) or [check a GPX file](https://whereday.com/tools/gpx-viewer/).

## [KML ⇄ GPX converter](https://whereday.com/tools/kml-to-gpx/)

Convert KML to GPX or GPX to KML, including Google Earth `gx:Track` timestamps, with a report of what was kept and skipped before you download. Your file stays in your browser.

Then [check the result in the GPX viewer](https://whereday.com/tools/gpx-viewer/).

## [Days-in-country calculator](https://whereday.com/tools/country-days-calculator/)

Count the days you spent in each country from a Whereday Country Days CSV or stays you type in. Check the Schengen 90/180 rule, any rolling window and calendar-year 183-day totals.

Expand each count to see its dates. [See how Whereday records country days](https://whereday.com/blog/track-days-in-country-iphone/).

## [Substantial presence test calculator](https://whereday.com/tools/substantial-presence-test-calculator/)

Apply the IRS weighted three-year formula (31 days this year, 183 weighted days) to a Country Days CSV, US stays you type in or yearly counts. See the weight for each year, the total and which dates were counted. This is a count, not tax advice. Your file stays in your browser.

Then [count days in other countries](https://whereday.com/tools/country-days-calculator/).

## [GPX viewer and file inspector](https://whereday.com/tools/gpx-viewer/)

Plot tracks and routes, check timestamps and invalid coordinates, and save CSV or GeoJSON with documented field limits. Your chosen file stays in your browser.

[Follow the worked inspection tutorial](https://whereday.com/blog/check-gpx-file/) with an invented five-point example.

Have a different file type? [Compare GPX, KML, GeoJSON and CSV](https://whereday.com/blog/gpx-kml-geojson-location-history/) before choosing a destination. The current viewer accepts GPX; renaming a file does not convert it.

## [Google Timeline JSON inspector](https://whereday.com/tools/google-timeline-inspector/)

Identify supported raw, semantic and device Timeline structures. Check coordinates and times, inspect findings and try invented examples before opening your own archive.

[Learn which Timeline JSON file you have](https://whereday.com/blog/google-timeline-json-formats/) and why browser counts can differ from a native import preview. Your selected file stays in the tool page.
