# Life360 location history: how far back it goes

How many days of history Life360 shows on each plan, why you may see gaps or only 2 days, what it documents about exports, and how to keep a longer record of your own days.

By [Cody Bontecou](https://isolated.tech), Whereday’s developer · Reviewed November 27, 2026

## Short answer

Life360 shows a rolling window of recent days, and older days drop off. According to Life360’s plans page, that window is 2 days on the free plan, 7 days on Silver and 30 days on Gold and Platinum. If you only see 2 days, you’re most likely on the free plan.

Life360 is built for sharing location inside a Circle, not as a personal archive. If you want a longer record of your own days, you have to record it yourself, as it happens.

## How far back Life360 history goes, by plan

Location History shown, as listed on Life360’s plans page
| Plan | History shown
| Free | 2 days
| Silver | 7 days
| Gold | 30 days
| Platinum | 30 days

Life360’s own article [How to See Location History](https://www.life360.com/blog/how-to-track-location-history) also says the free plan shows up to 2 days and Gold and Platinum show up to 30 days. Plan names, prices and what’s included vary by country, so check [Life360’s plans page](https://www.life360.com/plans-pricing) for what applies to you. This post leaves prices out on purpose.

Checked November 27, 2026 against Life360’s plans page and its blog article above. Life360 can change plans at any time.

## How to see location history in Life360

Life360’s article describes the steps like this: open your Circle, tap the name of a Circle member, then scroll down to see their recent places and trips for the days your plan allows. A member only shows up in this view if they have chosen to share their location with the Circle.

- Open Life360 and pick the Circle.
- Tap the name of the member whose own history you’re allowed to see, including your own name.
- Scroll down to the history section. Days outside your plan’s window aren’t shown.

Menu names change between app versions, so follow what the app shows. Source: Life360’s [How to See Location History](https://www.life360.com/blog/how-to-track-location-history), checked November 27, 2026. Life360 also publishes a support article, [Tile Location History in the Life360 app](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/32220552743959-Tile-Location-History-in-the-Life360-app), which covers Tile items rather than people.

Only look at a person’s history when they know their Circle can see it. This post doesn’t cover ways around that.

## Why history has gaps or only shows 2 days

- The plan window. The free plan shows 2 days, so anything older isn’t shown. See [the table above](https://whereday.com/blog/life360-location-history/#by-plan).
- Location sharing was off. Life360’s article says you can’t see a person’s whole history if they periodically turn location sharing off, and that history depends on location permission being set to “Always”.
- General iPhone causes (not specific to Life360). Background App Refresh turned off, Low Power Mode, or no signal can also leave holes in any app’s record. Life360 doesn’t document these in the sources we checked, so treat them as general iPhone behaviour. For the iPhone side, see [why location history is missing on iPhone](https://whereday.com/blog/missing-location-history-iphone/).

Source for the first two: Life360’s [How to See Location History](https://www.life360.com/blog/how-to-track-location-history), checked November 27, 2026.

## Can you save or export Life360 history?

Life360 does not document an in-app export of location history in the pages we checked: its plans page, its blog article and its App Store listing. We found no steps for downloading it as a file, so we won’t invent any.

For questions about the personal data Life360 holds about you, and any request you can make about it, read [Life360’s privacy policy](https://www.life360.com/privacy-policy/). ## Common questions

### Does Life360 show location history on the free plan?

Yes, for 2 days, according to [Life360’s plans page](https://www.life360.com/plans-pricing).

### Why does my Life360 history only go back 2 days?

That’s the free plan’s window. Silver shows 7 days and Gold and Platinum show 30, per the same page.

### Why are there gaps in someone’s Life360 history?

Life360 says history can’t be complete if location sharing is turned off from time to time. See [the gaps section](https://whereday.com/blog/life360-location-history/#gaps).

### Can I move my Life360 history into Whereday?

No. Whereday does not import Life360 data. It starts recording your own days from when you begin tracking.
