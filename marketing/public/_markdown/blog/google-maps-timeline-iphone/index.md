# Google Maps Timeline on iPhone: where it is and how to keep it

Where Timeline is on iPhone now, why it looks empty, and how to back it up, move it and export it.

By [Cody Bontecou](https://isolated.tech), Whereday’s developer · Reviewed November 22, 2026

## Where Google Maps Timeline is on iPhone

Open Google Maps, tap your profile picture or initial, then tap Your Timeline. That screen shows what Google Maps on this iPhone saved while Timeline was on. If Timeline was off, or the data was auto-deleted, deleted by you, or lives on another device, those days will be empty here.

The rest of this guide covers why Timeline often looks different from what you remember, how to turn it on, what to check when days are missing, how to back it up and move it to a new iPhone, and how to export a copy you control.

## Why your Timeline looks different or empty

In its [December 12, 2023 announcement](https://blog.google/products/maps/updates-to-location-history-and-new-controls-coming-soon-to-maps/), Google said three things about Timeline (then still called Location History):

- Saved on the device. Timeline would be saved on your device instead of only in the cloud. This applies to people who have turned the setting on; it is off by default.
- Optional encrypted backup. Backing up to the cloud is optional, useful when you switch phones. Google said backed-up data is encrypted so no one can read it, including Google.
- Three-month default auto-delete. When you first turn it on, auto-delete is set to three months by default, down from 18 months. Older data is removed automatically unless you extend the period or turn auto-delete off.

Google said the changes would roll out gradually on Android and iOS. So what you see depends on this phone’s settings, whether a backup exists, and your auto-delete period. This guide limits itself to what Google’s own pages say.

## Turn Timeline on

- Open Google Maps, tap your profile picture or initial, then Your Timeline.
- Tap More, then Location & privacy settings.
- Set Location to Always.
- Under Location settings, tap Timeline is off, then Turn on, then Turn on again.

Turning Timeline on now does not fill in past days. It saves from this point forward.

Steps follow [Google Maps Help: Manage your Timeline (iOS)](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6258979?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS), checked November 21, 2026. Google can change labels during rollouts; if yours differ, follow the current help page.

## Timeline is empty or missing days: checks

Causes that Google’s help page supports
| Situation | What to check | Where
| Timeline was off | Days before you turned it on were never saved. | Your Timeline → More → Location & privacy settings
| Location wasn’t Always | Set Location to Always. | Location & privacy settings
| Auto-delete removed older data | Auto-delete can be 3, 18 or 36 months; older data is gone. | Timeline settings
| Edits on another device | Edits and deletions on one device don’t carry over to others. | Check the device that recorded the day
| Maps deleted or device reset | Deleting Google Maps or resetting the device removes Timeline data from that device. | Restore from a backup if one exists
| New phone | Timeline doesn’t appear until you import from a backup. | See [backup](https://whereday.com/blog/google-maps-timeline-iphone/#backup) below

Days that were never recorded, or were deleted, can’t be recovered by Google or by Whereday.

## Back up Timeline and move it to a new iPhone

### Back up

- In Your Timeline, tap the cloud icon.
- If auto-delete is on, tap Don’t auto delete activity. Backup requires auto-delete to be off.
- Turn on Backup, then tap Yes.

Changes can take a few days to appear in a backup.

### Move to a new iPhone

- On the new device, open Your Timeline and tap the cloud icon.
- Under “Your backups”, select the old device.
- Tap More, then Import, then Import again.

Turning off backups doesn’t delete data already on your devices. Source: [Google Maps Help](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6258979?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS), checked November 21, 2026; labels may change.

## Export a copy you control

- In Google Maps, tap your profile picture or initial.
- Go to Settings → Location & Privacy → Export Timeline data.
- Choose Save to Files. The file is named `location-history.json`.

Keep an unchanged original and work on copies. To see what is inside, read the [Google Timeline JSON formats guide](https://whereday.com/blog/google-timeline-json-formats/) and open a copy in the [Timeline inspector](https://whereday.com/tools/google-timeline-inspector/), which runs in your browser.

## Delete a visit, a day, a range or everything

Google’s help page describes deleting a single visit, a day, a date range, or all of your Timeline, and Google’s 2023 post says you can delete all or part of your data at any time. Follow the Delete steps in [Manage your Timeline (iOS)](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6258979?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS) for the current menus on your version.

Deletion is permanent. Neither Google nor Whereday can bring deleted days back. If you might want them, export a copy or back up first.

## FAQ

### Can I see Google Timeline on a computer?

The help page we checked documents the iPhone steps, so those above are the documented path here. We don’t make claims about a computer view.

### Can I recover deleted Timeline data?

No. Deletion is permanent. A backup you made earlier is the only restore route Google describes ([see above](https://whereday.com/blog/google-maps-timeline-iphone/#backup)).

### Is Timeline the same as Significant Locations?

No. Timeline is Google Maps’ feature; Significant Locations is an Apple setting. See [the Significant Locations guide](https://whereday.com/blog/significant-locations-iphone/).
