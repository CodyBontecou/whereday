Getting started

Everything from installing Whereday to your first recorded day — what each permission is for and where your data lives from minute one.

Install and open

Whereday is a free download on the App Store. There is no account to create and nothing to sign up for — the first thing you see is a five-step introduction.

The onboarding steps

  1. Welcome — a short overview of what Whereday records and where it keeps it.
  2. Core features — visit timeline, route recording, and exports at a glance.
  3. Permission setup — Whereday asks for While Using location access first, then offers Always. Always is what enables automatic visit detection while the app is in the background; you can decline and still use manual tracking. If permission is denied, the app links you straight to iOS Settings.
  4. Connect Photos (optional) — you can enable photo moments now or skip entirely. Skipping changes nothing else.
  5. You're all set — optionally tick Start tracking immediately and Whereday begins recording the moment onboarding finishes.

After onboarding

You land on the Map: a map of today with your route, your visits, and a day card at the bottom. The other tabs are Atlas (your days at a glance), Places (your saved places), and Archive (export and import); Settings is the gear in Archive. The recording bar above the day card starts and stops both GPS recording and visit monitoring. With Always permission, recording can continue in the background. Installing the app does not reconstruct earlier history.

Your first day

  • Start tracking from the recording bar on the map before a walk or drive — you'll see the path draw in, and a Live Activity appears on your lock screen.
  • Stop tracking from the recording bar, the Live Activity's Stop button, Siri, or automatically with the auto-stop timer.
  • Swipe up the day card to review where you went, how long you stayed, and how far you moved. Tap the small dial at the top right of the map to replay the day.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. See the Privacy & your data guide for the complete picture of storage, network requests, and deletion.
