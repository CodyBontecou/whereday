Quick answer: where was I on a certain date?

  • Apple Maps: open Maps → Places → Visited Places and filter by date (iOS 26, where available). It only holds days it was turned on for and that your Keep Visits setting still retains.
  • Google Maps: if you used Timeline, look for it in the Google Maps app and follow Google’s Timeline help to find the date.
  • Neither was on? Rebuild the day from records that carry a real date: photos, workout route maps, Calendar, Wallet and receipts. They show less than a route, but often enough to answer the question.

Nothing on an iPhone will reveal a day that was never recorded, and changing the phone’s clock doesn’t either (see why). This guide is for your own phone and your own history.

Apple and Google details are covered in how to see your location history on iPhone.

Apple Maps: filter Visited Places by date

  1. Open Maps and tap Places, then Visited Places.
  2. Filter by date. You can also filter by category or city, or tap All Visits.
  3. Open a visit to see what Maps saved for it.

The Keep Visits caveat: at the bottom of the list, Keep Visits sets how long Maps retains visits, so an older date may already be gone. Visited Places also needs to have been turned on; see how to turn it on, which only affects visits from then on.

Keeping the answer: there is no documented export, so a screenshot is the only way to keep it. A screenshot is a picture, not a timestamped route, so write the date next to it.

Sources: Apple’s Visited Places guide for iOS 26 and Clear location history in Maps. Significant Locations & Routes, the Settings feature behind it, has no documented date filter.

Google Maps: Timeline

If you used Google Maps Timeline, it is the other place that may hold a day-by-day record. Menus and retention settings change between versions, so follow Google’s Timeline help for how to open it and pick a date. We haven’t re-verified those steps for this page. If you want an independent copy of what Google has, our import guide shows how to save and preview a Timeline JSON file.

Rebuild a day from records you already have

If no location history covers the date, work through these. Each has a real date even if it has no route.

  1. Photos. Browse Photos to the date, then open a photo’s info to see whether it has a location.
  2. Workout route maps. Open the workout from that date in Fitness or Health. If it recorded a route, it may show a map.
  3. Calendar. Look at the date for events, addresses and travel time entries.
  4. Wallet. Look through your card’s transactions in Wallet for the date. You get a merchant and a time, not a coordinate.
  5. Receipts and messages. Search Mail and Messages for the date, a booking or a receipt.

Accuracy: a photo’s location is where the photo was taken, not where you were all day. A transaction names a merchant, which may be a head office or an online charge. A calendar entry is a plan. Put them together and say what each one shows rather than claiming a route.

See the iPhone User Guide chapters on Photos, Fitness and Wallet for the exact steps on your iOS version. For photos shown on a map after you start recording, see Photos on the map.

The “change the Date & Time” trick: don’t

Some pages suggest turning off Set Automatically and changing the date to see old history. Apple documents no feature that works this way. The clock only changes the time your phone believes it is; it doesn’t reveal records that weren’t saved or that were deleted. It can also cause trouble: sign-in and two-factor codes depend on accurate time, and syncing and secure connections can fail. Leave Set Automatically on.

Why the date is blank

  • The feature was off. Visited Places, Timeline or a recorder wasn’t on for that day.
  • Keep Visits removed it. Maps dropped older visits according to your setting.
  • Clear History. Clearing location history in Maps removes what it saved; see Apple’s guide.
  • A new phone without a restored backup or Timeline backup.

Common questions

Can I see where I was a year ago?

Only if something recorded it and still keeps it: Visited Places within your Keep Visits setting, Google Timeline if it was on, or photos and receipts from that day.

Does Find My show past locations?

No. It shows a current or last known location only. See what Find My keeps.

Can I see the exact time I arrived?

Not reliably from Apple’s Visited Places guide, which documents no arrival time. Photo and transaction timestamps give times for those moments only. A Whereday visit shows its arrival and departure.

Can I export one day?

Apple’s features have no documented export, and Google’s export is a Timeline file, not one day. In Whereday you can set the export date range to a single day.

Can I see history for a date before I installed an app?

No. A new app starts from install. It can’t read Apple’s or Google’s history; it can import a Google Timeline file you exported yourself.
